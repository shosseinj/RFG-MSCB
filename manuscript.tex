\begin{abstract}
Accurate colorectal polyp segmentation remains challenging because lesions vary in scale, contrast, and boundary appearance. We present a ConvNeXt-Tiny U-shaped framework with bottleneck Frequency-Aware Feature Enhancement (FAFEM) and residual frequency-guided coupling to a Multi-Scale Convolution Block (MSCB). The resulting RFG-MSCB uses FAFEM's low- and high-frequency descriptor to weight multi-kernel branches at the Stage-3 encoder--decoder fusion while preserving ordinary MSCB aggregation as a reference. Under a PraNet-style protocol, checkpoint selection uses only an internal validation subset, with Kvasir-SEG and CVC-ClinicDB subsets reserved for final seen-domain testing and three datasets used for external testing. The model achieved mDice/mIoU of 0.932/0.883 on Kvasir-SEG and 0.948/0.904 on CVC-ClinicDB; external-set mDice ranged from 0.816 to 0.916. Across the five datasets, Residual RFG-MSCB increased aggregate mDice from 0.869 to 0.887 over FAFEM + MSCB while adding 0.08 M parameters, with no additional GFLOPs at the displayed precision.
\end{abstract}
