\section{Discussion}\label{discussion}

\subsection{Quantitative and Ablation Interpretation}

The model obtained higher scores on the two held-out validation subsets used for
checkpoint selection than on CVC-ColonDB and ETIS-LaribPolypDB. The complete
numerical results and uncertainty estimates are reported in the quantitative-analysis
subsection and comparison tables. This pattern is consistent with the architectural
motivation: the model retains a convolutional multi-scale pathway for efficient
decoding while adding sample-dependent guidance intended to adapt that pathway to
image content.

The ablation provides a more specific interpretation of the contribution. FAFEM
establishes the bottleneck feature-enhancement reference, MSCB-lite adds multi-scale
refinement at Stage 3 without frequency guidance, and RFG-MSCB adds adaptive branch
modulation to that reference path. The final increment is small, indicating that the
proposed coupling is a controlled refinement rather than a wholesale change in the
decoder. The paired single-seed comparison with direct frequency guidance also did
not show an advantage without residual coupling; consequently, the available evidence
does not disentangle residualization from frequency conditioning. These observations
support the feasibility of the combined mechanism but leave its individual causal
components unresolved.

The bounded residual formulation may explain why the added mechanism can be introduced
without discarding the ordinary multi-scale response. By interpolating between uniform
and predicted branch weights, it provides a conservative path from the established
MSCB behavior to sample-dependent refinement. This is a post hoc interpretation rather
than a separately tested mechanism-level conclusion, because the ablation measures the
combined design.

\subsection{Cross-Dataset Generalization}

Independent generalization is assessed on CVC-300, CVC-ColonDB, and
ETIS-LaribPolypDB, which did not influence checkpoint selection. Their lower scores
may reflect differences in acquisition, annotation, case mix, or polyp morphology
relative to the training data. The guidance change is also not
uniformly positive across the external datasets in the paired single-seed evidence.
Thus, the results support transfer across the evaluated datasets only in a limited,
descriptive sense and do not establish broad out-of-distribution generalization.

The weaker behavior on CVC-ColonDB and ETIS-LaribPolypDB, together with the observed
variability on ETIS, suggests that domain adaptation or targeted augmentation may be
needed. The model also does not outperform several recent methods on these datasets,
which reinforces that the proposed frequency-guided refinement does not remove the
underlying domain-shift challenge.

\subsection{Practical Trade-offs and Placement}

As summarized in Table~\ref{tab:model-complexity}, the final architecture has a
moderate parameter count within the verifiable subset. Computational-cost rankings
remain uncertain because most competitor reports do not provide a verified input
resolution together with an explicit FLOP-counting convention. No matched-hardware
latency benchmark was performed, so the present evidence does not establish real-time
inference suitability.

The final design keeps the new guidance localized to the Stage-3 decoder--encoder
fusion. The exploratory all-skip
comparison was single-seed, incomplete, and used a different batch size, so it does
not support a general conclusion about optimal placement. Together, the complexity and
placement results describe a constrained architectural trade-off, but they do not
establish the best deployment configuration.

These interpretive limits motivate the following limitations and future-work priorities.

\subsection{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{No statistical significance testing:} All reported differences are descriptive; no hypothesis testing was performed.
\item \textbf{Single-seed placement evidence:} The exploratory all-skip placement run is single-seed, incomplete, and uses a different batch size; it cannot establish a general placement rule.
\item \textbf{No disentanglement of RFG-MSCB components:} The ablation isolates the combined residual frequency-guided coupling; individual contributions of the residual formulation, initialization strength, and frequency conditioning are not separately measured.
\item \textbf{Incomplete experiments:} The residual-guidance run with default initialization did not complete training; its potential findings are unavailable.
\item \textbf{Domain gap on external datasets:} Performance drops substantially on CVC-ColonDB and ETIS-LaribPolypDB, indicating limited out-of-distribution generalization.
\item \textbf{Hardware and software not fully verified:} GPU model, CUDA/cuDNN versions, and \texttt{py\_sod\_metrics} version are not formally recorded.
\item \textbf{Training duration:} With zero-based epoch indices, seed 7777 stopped after epoch 167 (168 completed epochs; patience 30), while seeds 42 and 6543 completed epochs 0--199; this asymmetry may affect the mean estimate.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Future Work}

\begin{itemize}
\item Systematic multi-seed placement ablation with controlled batch size and learning rate.
\item Disentanglement of the RFG-MSCB components (residual form, initialization schedule, frequency conditioning) via factorial experiments.
\item Investigation of initialization sensitivity using completed default-initialization runs.
\item Domain adaptation or targeted augmentation to improve generalization on CVC-ColonDB and ETIS-LaribPolypDB.
\item Statistical significance testing with larger seed ensembles or bootstrap confidence intervals.
\item Formal recording of hardware, software, and dependency versions for full reproducibility.
\end{itemize}
