\section{Discussion}\label{discussion}

\subsection{Component Contributions}

The evaluation protocol separates seen-domain testing on Kvasir-SEG and
CVC-ClinicDB from external testing on CVC-300, CVC-ColonDB, and
ETIS-LaribPolypDB. All five test sets are isolated from checkpoint selection, which
uses only the internal validation subset of the combined development pool.

The controlled ablation evaluates Baseline, FAFEM, FAFEM + MSCB, and Residual RFG-MSCB
using unweighted five-dataset macro averages and three training seeds. FAFEM
raises mean mDice from 0.827 to 0.864, a 3.7-percentage-point increase, and
adding MSCB raises it by a further 0.3 points to 0.867. Residual RFG-MSCB then
adds 0.5 points to reach 0.872 mDice and has the highest means for mDice, mIoU,
$F_{\beta}^{w}$, $S_{\alpha}$, $mE_{\phi}$, and $\max E_{\phi}$. The displayed
MAE decreases from 0.019 to 0.018 with FAFEM and remains tied at 0.018 for the
two subsequent configurations. These descriptive aggregate changes support the
combined residual frequency-guided formulation at the Stage-3 fusion without
establishing improvement for every image or statistical significance.

The bounded residual design preserves the conventional MSCB response while allowing
sample-dependent branch modulation. Because residualization and frequency conditioning
were not evaluated independently, their individual contributions remain unresolved.
This adaptive refinement reuses the bottleneck descriptor at one decoder stage without
discarding the established multi-scale response or modifying the full decoder.

\subsection{Cross-Dataset Generalization}

CVC-300, CVC-ColonDB, and ETIS-LaribPolypDB are reserved for external
generalization evaluation and do not influence model development. Differences in
acquisition, annotation, case mix, and polyp morphology make these datasets a distinct
test of transfer beyond the Kvasir-SEG/CVC-ClinicDB development domain.

On the external datasets, the model obtained mDice/mIoU of 0.916/0.863 on
CVC-300, 0.824/0.747 on CVC-ColonDB, and 0.816/0.763 on ETIS-LaribPolypDB.
Performance varied across these datasets, with the strongest transfer observed on
CVC-300. On CVC-ColonDB and ETIS, the model's mIoU was second-highest among the
displayed methods, whereas its mDice was below the two highest displayed values.
Differences in acquisition, annotation, and lesion appearance are plausible contributors
to the lower CVC-ColonDB and ETIS results, but were not isolated experimentally.

Although the main comparison is restricted to methods with closely matched dataset
partitioning, differences in optimization, augmentation, backbone initialization,
model-selection criteria, and implementation details remain.

\subsection{Practical Trade-offs and Placement}

As summarized in Table~\ref{tab:model-complexity}, the final architecture has
30.057 M parameters and requires 27.818 GFLOPs at $352\times352$. The matched-hardware
results in Table~\ref{tab:ablation} show modest runtime and memory overhead for the
residual frequency-guided extension relative to FAFEM + MSCB. The Stage-3 localization is also consistent
with the MSCB-lite placement analysis, where Stage 3 gave the best observed
accuracy--complexity trade-off and extending refinement to additional skip levels
increased computational cost without improving the five-dataset macro-average overlap
metrics. This provides empirical motivation for Stage-3 localization but does not
establish the optimal placement of Residual RFG-MSCB, which was not exhaustively
placement-tested. Deployment-oriented latency optimization remains future work.

\subsection{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{No statistical significance testing:} All reported differences are descriptive; no hypothesis testing was performed.
\item \textbf{Seed coverage:} The Table~\ref{tab:ablation} comparisons use three training seeds, which limits characterization of run-to-run variability.
\item \textbf{Component isolation:} Residualization and frequency conditioning were not independently disentangled.
\item \textbf{Placement evaluation:} MSCB-lite placement was evaluated across the reported seeds, whereas Residual RFG-MSCB placement was not exhaustively tested across decoder stages or multiple seeds.
\item \textbf{Runtime evaluation:} Runtime was measured on one GPU under one FP16, batch-size-1 inference setting; broader deployment evaluation across hardware platforms remains future work.
\item \textbf{External generalization:} Performance was lower on CVC-ColonDB and ETIS-LaribPolypDB than on CVC-300, motivating broader evaluation across acquisition domains.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Future Work}

\begin{itemize}
\item Systematic multi-seed placement evaluation of Residual RFG-MSCB with controlled batch size and learning rate.
\item Disentanglement of residualization, frequency conditioning, and initialization through factorial experiments.
\item Domain adaptation or targeted augmentation for CVC-ColonDB and ETIS-LaribPolypDB.
\item Evaluation of between-configuration differences using larger seed ensembles or bootstrap confidence intervals.
\end{itemize}
