\section{Conclusion}\label{conclusion}

We presented a ConvNeXt-Tiny U-shaped segmentation framework that couples a locally
implemented bottleneck FAFEM descriptor to MSCB through RFG-MSCB at the Stage-3
encoder--decoder fusion. The design extends frequency enhancement into decoder
refinement while preserving the ordinary multi-scale pathway as a reference.

The final model achieved mDice/mIoU of 0.932/0.883 on Kvasir-SEG and transferred
most strongly to CVC-300 among the three external datasets, with lower performance on
CVC-ColonDB and ETIS-LaribPolypDB. Across the five-dataset aggregate, Residual
RFG-MSCB increased mDice from 0.869 to 0.887 over FAFEM + MSCB. The localized
modification adds 0.08 M parameters without additional reported GFLOPs at the displayed
precision and leaves the remaining depthwise-separable decoder stages unchanged.

Future work should evaluate alternative placements across multiple seeds, disentangle
residualization from frequency conditioning, and examine adaptation across acquisition
domains.
