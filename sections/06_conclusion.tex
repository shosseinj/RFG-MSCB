\section{Conclusion}\label{conclusion}

Taken together, the experiments support a cautious conclusion. We presented a
ConvNeXt-Tiny U-shaped segmentation framework that couples prior FAFEM
and MSCB modules through RFG-MSCB at the Stage-3 encoder--decoder fusion. The design
extends bottleneck frequency enhancement into decoder refinement while preserving the
ordinary multi-scale pathway as a reference, thereby addressing the motivation for
sample-dependent receptive-field selection without replacing the established decoder
path.

The empirical evidence supports a cautious interpretation. The ablation indicates
that the added residual frequency guidance produces a small change relative to the
FAFEM + MSCB reference, while the external-dataset results are less consistent than
the held-out results. The complexity comparison and localized placement describe a
moderate architectural trade-off, but the available experiments do not establish
uniform gains, component-level causality, or real-time suitability.

These findings motivate systematic multi-seed placement experiments, disentanglement
of residualization and frequency conditioning, and domain-adaptation strategies for
the more challenging external datasets. The incomplete default-initialization run,
single-seed placement evidence, and absence of significance testing should be resolved
before making stronger claims about generalization or superiority.
