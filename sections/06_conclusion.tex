\section{Conclusion}\label{conclusion}

We presented a ConvNeXt-Tiny U-shaped segmentation framework that couples a locally
implemented bottleneck FAFEM descriptor to MSCB through RFG-MSCB at the Stage-3
encoder--decoder fusion. The design extends frequency enhancement into decoder
refinement while preserving the ordinary multi-scale pathway as a reference.

The final model obtained mDice/mIoU of 0.932/0.883 on Kvasir-SEG and
0.948/0.904 on CVC-ClinicDB, compared with 0.916/0.863 on CVC-300,
0.824/0.747 on CVC-ColonDB, and 0.816/0.763 on ETIS-LaribPolypDB.
The parameter-efficient modification is localized to Stage 3, adds 0.08 M parameters
over FAFEM + MSCB without additional reported GFLOPs at the displayed precision,
and retains depthwise-separable convolution elsewhere. In the ablation
averaged across all five datasets, Residual RFG-MSCB achieved mDice/mIoU/MAE of
0.887/0.832/0.014, compared with 0.869/0.803/0.018 for FAFEM + MSCB.

Future work should examine placement across multiple seeds, separate residualization
from frequency conditioning, and evaluate domain-adaptation strategies for the
external datasets. Overall, RFG-MSCB connects bottleneck frequency
information to sample-dependent multi-scale decoding through bounded residual coupling
that preserves the original MSCB pathway.
