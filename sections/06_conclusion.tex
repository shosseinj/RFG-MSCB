\section{Conclusion}\label{conclusion}

We presented a ConvNeXt-Tiny U-shaped segmentation framework that couples a locally
implemented bottleneck FAFEM descriptor to MSCB through RFG-MSCB at the Stage-3
encoder--decoder fusion. The design extends frequency enhancement into decoder
refinement while preserving the ordinary multi-scale pathway as a reference.

The final model achieved mDice/mIoU of 0.932/0.883 on Kvasir-SEG and transferred
most strongly to CVC-300 among the three external datasets, with lower performance on
CVC-ColonDB and ETIS-LaribPolypDB. In the controlled ablation, FAFEM + MSCB reached
0.867 mDice before Residual RFG-MSCB increased the mean to 0.872 and achieved the
highest means across all six positive-valued aggregate metrics. The displayed MAE
remained 0.018 after the FAFEM stage. The final modification adds 0.08 M parameters
and 0.304 GFLOPs over FAFEM + MSCB without changing the remaining depthwise-separable
decoder stages.

Future work should evaluate Residual RFG-MSCB placement across multiple seeds,
disentangle residualization from frequency conditioning, and examine adaptation across
acquisition domains.
