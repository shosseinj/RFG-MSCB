\section{Ablation Study}\label{ablation-study}

We compare three configurations under the common experimental protocol: FAFEM,
FAFEM + MSCB, and FAFEM + RFG-MSCB. The sequence provides protocol-matched references
for the additions made after FAFEM, but it does not measure FAFEM against a no-FAFEM
model under the final protocol or independently isolate all components of RFG-MSCB.

\subsection{Ablation Results}

The sequence begins with FAFEM as the bottleneck enhancement module. The FAFEM-only
configuration contains no multi-scale refinement layers and provides the final-protocol
reference. Older no-FAFEM measurements used a different protocol and are excluded
from this comparison.

Adding a Stage-3 MSCB-lite layer introduces parallel multi-scale refinement without
frequency guidance. This configuration supplies the appropriate reference for the
proposed guided variant because it preserves the multi-scale path while omitting the
cross-level conditioning. The final configuration then adds residual frequency-guided
coupling to that Stage-3 layer. The complete numerical comparison is given in
Table~\ref{tab:ablation}.

A single-seed direct frequency-guidance configuration tested the mechanism without
residual coupling. Its Kvasir-SEG result was lower than that of Stage-3 MSCB-lite in
the paired seed-42 comparison.

\subsection{Ablation Interpretation}

FAFEM provides the bottleneck feature-enhancement reference. MSCB-lite adds
multi-scale refinement at Stage 3, and RFG-MSCB introduces frequency-conditioned
adaptive branch modulation along this path. The final gain is small and is accompanied
by the parameter increase reported in Table~\ref{tab:ablation}.

\subsection{Stage Placement and Generalization}

Stage 3 is used in the selected configuration. The exploratory all-skip
experiment used a single seed, was incomplete, and used batch size 32 with the same
learning rate. Its training result was lower than that of the Stage-3 configuration.
A controlled multi-seed placement study remains future work.

On the four additional datasets, the change from FAFEM + MSCB to the final guidance
configuration was mixed in the paired seed-42 comparison: mDice decreased on
CVC-ClinicDB and CVC-300 and increased on CVC-ColonDB and ETIS. The residual-coupling configuration
with default initialization did not complete training and is therefore excluded from
the quantitative ablation; initialization sensitivity remains a subject for future
work.
