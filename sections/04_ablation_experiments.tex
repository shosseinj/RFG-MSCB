\section{Ablation Study}\label{ablation-study}

We perform ablation studies to quantify the effect of our proposed frequency-guided residual coupling mechanism when added to the FAFEM-enhanced backbone. We also analyze the placement of the multi-scale refinement layer.

The ablation experiments use the same training and evaluation protocol as the final FAFEM + RFG-MSCB model, with variations in the architecture only. Unless otherwise noted, results are reported as mean $\pm$ standard deviation over three random seeds.

\subsection{Effect of FAFEM}

We first establish the FAFEM-only configuration, which contains no multi-scale refinement layers. Its three-seed Kvasir-SEG results are 0.925958 mDice, 0.875879 mIoU, and 0.024832 MAE. FAFEM is prior work, not a contribution of this paper. A no-FAFEM baseline under the final training protocol is not available; the older baseline measurements are protocol-mismatched and are therefore not used for a quantitative final-protocol comparison.

\subsection{Effect of Stage-3 MSCB-Lite}

Adding a Stage-3 MSCB-lite layer to the FAFEM-enhanced backbone gives the FAFEM + MSCB configuration. MSCB-lite is prior multi-scale refinement without frequency-guided coupling. The three-seed Kvasir-SEG mDice changes from 0.925958 to 0.930440 (+0.448 percentage points); mIoU changes from 0.875879 to 0.881375 (+0.550 percentage points), and MAE changes from 0.024832 to 0.024487 (-0.000345). This configuration establishes the baseline for the frequency-guided variant.

\subsection{Effect of Residual Frequency-Guided Coupling}

The final FAFEM + RFG-MSCB model adds residual frequency-guided coupling to the Stage-3 multi-scale layer. This is the principal architecture change relative to the FAFEM + MSCB configuration. Using the raw three-seed values, Kvasir-SEG mDice changes from 0.930440 to 0.931284 (+0.084 percentage points), mIoU changes from 0.881375 to 0.882594 (+0.122 percentage points), and MAE changes from 0.024487 to 0.023960 (-0.000527). The small descriptive differences should not be interpreted as evidence of statistical significance.

A single-seed direct frequency-guidance configuration tested the mechanism without residual coupling; its Kvasir-SEG result did not exceed the Stage-3 MSCB-lite result in that paired seed-42 comparison. Because it is single-seed, this result does not isolate the independent effect of residualization.

\subsection{Stage Placement Analysis}

The placement of the multi-scale refinement layer is analyzed experimentally. In the final FAFEM + RFG-MSCB configuration, the layer is applied only at encoder Stage 3. This placement was selected based on observed results rather than theoretical assumptions.

A single-seed exploratory all-skip configuration used batch size 32 and the same learning rate; the available training evidence did not improve relative to the Stage-3 configuration. However, this comparison is limited by incomplete training and the batch-size mismatch, so we cannot draw general conclusions about optimal placement from it. Future work could systematically ablate stage placements with more rigorous statistical verification.

\subsection{Ablation Discussion}

Our three-seed ablation shows that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item FAFEM provides the verified feature-enhanced baseline (0.9260 mDice on Kvasir-SEG).
\item Adding MSCB-lite at Stage 3 changes mDice to 0.9304, a +0.448 percentage-point difference relative to FAFEM.
\item Incorporating residual frequency-guided coupling changes mDice to 0.9313, a +0.084 percentage-point difference relative to FAFEM + MSCB; this is a descriptive three-seed result, not a significance claim.
\end{enumerate}

The parameter count increases from 29.4M for FAFEM to 30.0M for FAFEM + MSCB and 30.1M for the final FAFEM + RFG-MSCB model.

\subsection{Generalization Evaluation}

The final RFG-MSCB model is evaluated on the four additional datasets, but its change relative to FAFEM + MSCB is mixed: paired seed-42 mDice decreases on CVC-ClinicDB and CVC-300 and increases on CVC-ColonDB and ETIS. Therefore, the ablation does not establish uniform cross-dataset improvement or broad generalization of the guidance mechanism.

The residual-coupling configuration with default initialization did not complete training, so it is excluded from the quantitative ablation. Future work may examine sensitivity to initialization strength in more detail.
