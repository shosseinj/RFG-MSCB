\section{Ablation Study}\label{ablation-study}

The ablation design compares three configurations in sequence: Baseline,
FAFEM + MSCB, and Residual RFG-MSCB (Ours). This sequence evaluates the
feature-enhancement and multi-scale components together before assessing the
additional residual frequency-guided modification.

\subsection{Ablation Results}

The Baseline serves as the reference configuration without FAFEM and MSCB. Adding
FAFEM and MSCB improves aggregate segmentation performance across the five evaluation
datasets. Residual RFG-MSCB then introduces frequency-guided coupling, allowing the
bottleneck descriptor to modulate the multi-scale branches at the Stage-3 decoder
fusion. Table~\ref{tab:ablation} summarizes the arithmetic mean of each segmentation
metric across Kvasir-SEG, CVC-ClinicDB, CVC-300, CVC-ColonDB, and
ETIS-LaribPolypDB together with the corresponding model complexity.

\subsection{Ablation Interpretation}

Across the five-dataset averages, FAFEM + MSCB increases mDice, mIoU,
$F_{\beta}^{w}$, $S_{\alpha}$, $mE_{\phi}$, and $\max E_{\phi}$ over the Baseline
by 0.042, 0.040, 0.034, 0.020, 0.030, and 0.034, respectively, corresponding to
gains of 4.2, 4.0, 3.4, 2.0, 3.0, and 3.4 percentage points. It reduces MAE by
0.001. Residual RFG-MSCB further increases these six metrics over FAFEM + MSCB
by 0.018, 0.029, 0.019, 0.013, 0.015, and 0.009, respectively (1.8, 2.9, 1.9,
1.3, 1.5, and 0.9 percentage points), and reduces MAE by 0.004. The parameter
count increases from 29.19 M to 29.98 M and then 30.06 M, changes of 0.79 M and
0.08 M, while GFLOPs increase from 27.202 to 27.818 and then remain unchanged,
changes of 0.616 and 0.000. These are descriptive comparisons; no statistical
significance is claimed.

\subsection{Qualitative Ablation Analysis}

Relative to the Baseline, FAFEM + MSCB produces mask shapes with closer spatial
coverage or boundary alignment in several displayed examples. In the displayed
CVC-ColonDB case with an elongated annotation, the disconnected Baseline regions are
replaced by a more coherent prediction. For the displayed small-lesion
ETIS-LaribPolypDB example, the Baseline does not recover the annotated region,
whereas both augmented configurations produce a localized response.

Residual RFG-MSCB yields a more coherent or better-localized mask in selected cases,
including the displayed CVC-ColonDB case, while its effect varies among the selected
images. In the displayed Kvasir-SEG, CVC-ClinicDB, and CVC-300 examples, the predictions
differ mainly in boundary position and extent, and no configuration reproduces every
ground-truth contour exactly. These examples therefore provide a visual complement to
the aggregate trends in Table~\ref{tab:ablation}, rather than evidence of uniform
improvement for every image.

\subsection{Stage Placement}

The selected configuration localizes RFG-MSCB to Stage 3. Systematic multi-seed
evaluation of alternative placements remains future work.
