\section{Ablation Study}\label{ablation-study}

The ablation design compares three configurations in sequence: Baseline,
FAFEM + MSCB, and Residual RFG-MSCB (Ours). This sequence evaluates the
feature-enhancement and multi-scale components together before assessing the
additional residual frequency-guided modification.

\subsection{Ablation Results}

The Baseline provides the reference without the added FAFEM and MSCB components.

Adding FAFEM and MSCB improves the baseline across the reported segmentation
metrics. The final configuration then adds residual frequency-guided coupling to
the multi-scale configuration. The complete numerical comparison, including
computational complexity, is given in Table~\ref{tab:ablation}.

A single-seed direct frequency-guidance configuration tested the mechanism without
residual coupling. Its Kvasir-SEG result was lower than that of FAFEM + MSCB in
the paired seed-42 comparison.

\subsection{Ablation Interpretation}

FAFEM + MSCB improves mDice from 0.827 to 0.869, mIoU from 0.763 to
0.803, and reduces MAE from 0.019 to 0.018 relative to the Baseline. Residual
RFG-MSCB further changes mDice from 0.869 to 0.875, mIoU from 0.803 to 0.815,
and MAE from 0.018 to 0.017. The parameter count increases from 29.19 M to
29.98 M and then 30.06 M, while GFLOPs increase from 27.202 to 27.818 and
remain 27.818 for the final configuration. Thus, the residual modification adds
only 0.08 M parameters over FAFEM + MSCB and no additional reported GFLOPs at
the displayed precision. These are descriptive comparisons; no statistical
significance is claimed.

Stage 3 is used in the selected configuration. The exploratory all-skip
experiment used a single seed, was incomplete, and used batch size 32 with the same
learning rate. Its training result was lower than that of the Stage-3 configuration.
A controlled multi-seed placement study remains future work.

On the four additional datasets, the change from FAFEM + MSCB to the final guidance
configuration was mixed in the paired seed-42 comparison: mDice decreased on
CVC-ClinicDB and CVC-300 and increased on CVC-ColonDB and ETIS. The residual-coupling configuration
with default initialization did not complete training and is therefore excluded from
the quantitative ablation; initialization sensitivity remains a subject for future
work.
